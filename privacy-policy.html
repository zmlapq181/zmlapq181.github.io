Sky Cake – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Sky Cake (Android package com.piotrowski.skycake), developed by Paweł Piotrowski ("I", "me"), handles information. Sky Cake has no user accounts and I do not collect, store or sell your personal information myself.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score locally on your phone. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sky Cake is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent and choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings link on the game's main menu.

On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

Sky Cake is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because I do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google through the links above. You can also contact me and I will help.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
